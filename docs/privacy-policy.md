# Chudbot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [MONTH DAY, YEAR]  
**Last updated:** [MONTH DAY, YEAR]

This Privacy Policy explains how **[OWNER/OPERATOR LEGAL NAME]** (“Chudbot,”
“we,” “us,” or “our”) processes information when you interact with Chudbot,
our Discord bot. Discord separately processes information under Discord’s own
Privacy Policy. Chudbot is a third-party application and is not operated by
Discord.

## 1. Information we process

Depending on the features enabled in a server, Chudbot may process:

- Discord identifiers, including user, guild/server, channel, message, role,
  and bot identifiers;
- Discord display information needed to respond, such as username, display
  name, member, role, and channel information;
- command and interaction information, including command arguments and
  settings selected by a user or server administrator;
- moderation information, including warning text and the identifiers of the
  relevant user and server;
- economy information, including balances, transactions, inventory, equipment,
  loans, market orders, game activity, rankings, and economy audit events;
- configuration information, such as channels selected for reaction boards,
  voice logs, deleted-message audit logs, and other server features;
- message information when required by enabled features. For example, the
  message-reward listener uses the author and server identifiers, and a
  configured deleted-message audit log may include message author, content,
  attachments, channel, and message identifiers;
- voice-event information, such as a member’s username and the voice channel
  joined or left, when voice logging is enabled; and
- technical information made available by Discord or infrastructure providers,
  such as request, error, and connection logs.

We do not intentionally request passwords, Discord tokens, payment-card data,
precise location, health information, or the contents of voice communications.
Music playback is handled through Lavalink; Chudbot does not intentionally
record voice audio.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- operate commands, games, moderation, economy, music, and server utilities;
- authenticate and enforce Discord permissions;
- maintain server configuration and user game/economy state;
- provide administrator-selected reaction, voice, and deleted-message logs;
- prevent abuse, investigate errors, maintain security, and enforce our Terms;
- communicate about the Service when necessary; and
- comply with legal obligations or protect rights, safety, and property.

We do not sell personal information, use Discord API data for targeted
advertising, or use message content to train machine-learning or AI models.

## 3. Legal bases

Where data-protection law such as the GDPR applies, we generally rely on:

- performance of the Service requested by a server administrator or user;
- legitimate interests in security, abuse prevention, debugging, and reliable
  operation; and
- compliance with legal obligations.

Where consent is legally required, we will request it or rely on the relevant
Discord/server administrator control. Server administrators should enable only
features appropriate for their community and should provide any notices their
own legal obligations require.

## 4. Sharing and service providers

We may disclose or transmit information to:

- Discord, as necessary for the bot to receive events and send responses;
- the operator’s hosting, logging, and PostgreSQL database providers;
- Lavalink or configured media providers for music features;
- FxTwitter when the link-preview feature checks an X/Twitter post for video;
- NASA, Minecraft server-status, or similar APIs when a user invokes those
  features; and
- authorities or other parties when required by law, to protect people or the
  Service, or to address fraud or abuse.

We do not sell, rent, or provide Discord API data to data brokers or advertising
networks. Providers may process information under their own policies and may
operate in countries other than yours.
